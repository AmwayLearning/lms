Module 6 - Nutrition and Health Trends - xAPI (Tin Can) package
---------------------------------------------------------------
Upload this zip as-is to any xAPI-capable LMS (tincan.xml is at the root).
Launch file: index.html
Thumbnail: assets/img/thumbnail.jpg

Activity ID: https://wiseprogramme.com/xapi/courses/nutrition-and-health-trends
(change it in tincan.xml and js/xapi.js -> COURSE.id if you need your own IRI)

Lessons (swipeable one-screen pages, based on the Module 6 slide deck, about 8 minutes plus videos)
  1. Weight Management (video + 6 screens + lesson complete)
  2. Gut Health (video + 5 screens + lesson complete)
  3. Cellular Health (video + 5 screens + pocket notes + lesson complete)
  Videos: Brightcove player 5048125494001/EECYz3XbU, ids 6408583339112, 6408583340112, 6408582162112 (statement: played).
  Pocket notes: 1 page, view and zoom only (no download), on the notes page and at the end of lesson 3
  PDF slides: downloadable (assets/docs/Module_6_Nutrition_and_Health_Trends_Slides.pdf).
              Amway+ link (copy-link box): https://ap.click.amway.com/CtiZiK4a (AMWAY_PLUS_URL in js/app.js)
  Knowledge check: 5 questions, no pass mark, open any time (lessons are optional)

The LMS passes endpoint, auth, actor, registration and activity_id on the
launch URL. Statements sent: initialized/resumed, experienced (each screen),
interacted, completed (each lesson), answered (each question),
completed (knowledge check, with score), completed (course, with score),
downloaded (PDF), terminated. Progress is saved with the xAPI State API so
learners resume on the screen they left.

Opened without an LRS, the course runs in preview mode and saves progress
in the browser only.
