Module 5 - Nutrition and NCDs - xAPI (Tin Can) package
------------------------------------------------------
Preventing non-communicable diseases through lifestyle management.

Upload this zip as-is to any xAPI-capable LMS (tincan.xml is at the root).
Launch file: index.html
Thumbnail: assets/img/thumbnail.jpg

Activity ID: https://wiseprogramme.com/xapi/courses/nutrition-and-ncds
(change it in tincan.xml and js/xapi.js -> COURSE.id if you need your own IRI)

Lessons (swipeable one-screen pages, based on the Module 5 slide deck, about 8 minutes plus videos)
  1. Introduction to NCDs (video + 5 screens + pocket notes p1 + lesson complete)
  2. Importance of Prevention (video + 3 screens + lesson complete)
  3. Strategies to Support NCDs Management (video + 5 screens + pocket notes p2 + lesson complete)
  Videos: Brightcove player 5048125494001/EECYz3XbU, video ids 6408324547112, 6408325606112, 6408326187112 (statement: played).
  Videos: Brightcove (account 5048125494001, player EECYz3XbU) ids 6408324547112, 6408325606112, 6408326187112. Statement: played.
  Pocket notes: 2 pages, view and zoom only (no download), on the notes page and inside lessons 1 and 3
  PDF slides: downloadable (assets/docs/Module_5_Nutrition_and_NCDs_Slides.pdf).
              Amway+ link (copy-link box): https://ap.click.amway.com/7KXqkjsJ (AMWAY_PLUS_URL in js/app.js)
  Knowledge check: 5 questions, no pass mark (unlocks after all 3 lessons)

The LMS passes endpoint, auth, actor, registration and activity_id on the
launch URL. Statements sent: initialized/resumed, experienced (each screen),
interacted, completed (each lesson), answered (each question),
completed (knowledge check, with score), completed (course, with score),
downloaded (PDF), terminated. Progress is saved with the xAPI State API so
learners resume on the screen they left.

Opened without an LRS, the course runs in preview mode and saves progress
in the browser only.
